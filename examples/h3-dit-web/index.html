The prompt list is fixed, and that is the honest shape of this. MiniMax-H3 conditions on Qwen3-VL-32B — 66.7 GB, against the DiT's 20.08 and the decoder's 2.43 — so the text encoder runs offline in tools/encode_prompt.py and this page reads the embeddings it wrote. A prompt nobody has encoded cannot be generated here.

This page holds about 23 GB on the GPU. 20.08 GB of int8 DiT weights, 0.58 GB of precomputed modulation tables and 2.43 GB of VAE decoder. int8 is what makes that number 23 and not 36; what it costs was measured, and the README has the three ways it was measured. Build .
