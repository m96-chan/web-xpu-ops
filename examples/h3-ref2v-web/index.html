Three models run in sequence, not together. The conditioner is 26.25 GB of int8, the DiT 20.08 GB and the VAE decoder 2.43 — 48.7 GB, which does not fit at once on any card this page will meet. Each is uploaded, used, and dropped before the next, so a generation pays about three uploads.

The reference is resized by the browser, not by PIL. Upstream resamples with PIL's bicubic and this page has drawImage. Everything downstream of that — the patchify, the tower, the layout, the sampler — is held to the model's own output; what the resampler itself costs is unmeasured. Build .
