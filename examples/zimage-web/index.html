Z-Image, in this browser

Prompt to picture on WebGPU — tokenizer, Qwen3-4B text encoder, the DiT and the VAE decoder, all composed from web-xpu-ops' kernels. Nothing is sent anywhere.

The model downloads once. 6.17 GB of the DiT is fetched into the browser's disk cache before the first generation; after that nothing is downloaded, on this visit or the next. It is deliberately not kept in memory — that left too little heap for the activations, and 512 × 512 failed to allocate.

The two checkpoints want different settings, and mixing them looks like a bug in the renderer. Base is undistilled: 28–50 steps with CFG 3–5. Turbo is distilled for 8 steps and does not use CFG. Running Base on Turbo's numbers produces a latent that has not converged — shattered-glass artefacts, and style words in the prompt ignored.

1024 is the size the model was trained at. CFG costs two forward passes per step, so Base at 1024 is about 24 s a step on the machine this was measured on. Measured numbers are in the example's README.
