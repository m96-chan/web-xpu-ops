Z-Image, in this browser

Prompt to picture on WebGPU — tokenizer, Qwen3-4B text encoder, the DiT and the VAE decoder, all composed from web-xpu-ops' kernels. Nothing is sent anywhere.

The model loads once. 6.17 GB of the DiT is fetched and held in memory before the first generation; after that nothing is downloaded, and each denoising step is pure compute. The text encoder is cached on its first prompt.

It is still not fast: weights are uploaded to the GPU per dispatch rather than kept resident, and attention grows quadratically with size. Measured numbers are in the example's README.
