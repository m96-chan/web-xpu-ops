MiniMax-H3's video decoder

The visual half of an omni-modal video model: 36 transformer blocks over a 24-channel latent, 16x in space and 4x in time. Checked against the model's own pixels to 5.8e-6.

There is no text prompt here, and that is not an omission. This is the decoder. The 20B transformer that would write the latent and the Qwen3-VL-32B encoder that would read a prompt are 27 GB between them and are not in this page. A latent sampled from the prior is what a VAE decoder is built to receive, so that is what it is given. The weights are 2.43 GB of int8 and stay on the GPU between decodes. int8 was measured against the model's own pixels at at most two levels of 255; the f32 conversion is 9.69 GB and is what the verification script checks.
